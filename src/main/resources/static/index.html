      Citizen Awareness

      Learn Common Scams in India

      Understand the deceptive mechanics behind phishing, digital arrest extortion, and rogue Android APKs.

      How Phishing Works

      Fraudsters mimic trusted banks (SBI, HDFC), payment apps (Paytm, PhonePe), or couriers to steal OTPs and credentials through fake links.

      • The Lure: Artificial panic like "account blocked in 24h" or "lottery reward".
      • The Fake Link: Typosquatted lookalike URLs (e.g. paytrn.com, sbi-verify.xyz).
      • The Theft: Harvesting OTPs, PINs, or CVVs to empty your bank accounts.

      How Digital Arrest Scams Work

      Scammers pose as CBI, Police, NCB, or Customs officers and falsely claim your Aadhaar or SIM was used in a drug parcel or money laundering case.

      • Video Call Con: Demands to "stay on Skype/WhatsApp video call" with fake police backdrops.
      • Secrecy: Strict orders of "do not tell anyone" to isolate victims from family and lawyers.
      • Extortion: Coercing victims to transfer money to a "safe government account" for verification.

      How to Spot Malicious Apps

      Attackers trick users into installing rogue APK files or remote desktop tools disguised as customer service, electricity, or KYC apps.

      • Sideloading: Urging you to "enable unknown sources" and install an .apk link.
      • Remote Access: Pushing AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport to take over your screen.
      • Dangerous Permissions: Requesting Accessibility or SMS permissions to steal incoming OTPs.

      Golden Rules Security Checklist

      Core safety principles to protect yourself and your family across every digital transaction:

      • Never share OTPs or PINs — Legitimate banks, RBI, and telecom operators NEVER ask for OTPs, CVVs, or ATM PINs.
      • Digital arrest is fake — Indian law enforcement (Police, CBI, ED, Courts) NEVER conducts arrests or trials over video calls.
      • Never install APK links — Only download software through official app stores (Google Play Store or Apple App Store).
      • Verify URL spelling — Always check the exact domain name character-by-character before entering credentials.
      • Pause on artificial urgency — Threats of electricity disconnection or immediate block are designed to trigger hasty mistakes.
      • Call verified numbers only — Dial the fraud helpline on the back of your physical debit card, never numbers found on Google.

      If you have been targeted, lost money, or suspect a scam, act immediately through official Government of India channels:

      Toll-Free 24x7
